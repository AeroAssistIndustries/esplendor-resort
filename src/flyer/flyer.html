The opportunity

A resort site with the hard part done

Esplendor opened in 1970 as a Spanish Colonial resort on a mesa with 360° views of the valley. A kitchen fire closed it in 2015. Since 2021 the owners have demolished and cleared the damaged areas, repaired roofs and corridors, and commissioned a complete remodeling plan set.

  • Plans done: architectural, structural, MEP, civil and water-line plans, approved by the fire department; Phase I ESA; title insurance; 39 renderings. Roof repairs complete.
  • Demand nearby: South32's Hermosa mine, about 15 miles away, plans 800–900 long-term jobs averaging about $90,000 a year.
  • No new supply: county hotel occupancy rose 8.8% in Q1 2026 with no new rooms added.
  • Room to grow: about 6.8 acres of open hillside plus two street lots for extended stay, casitas or events.
  • Border economy: 10 miles from the Nogales ports of entry and 50 minutes from Tucson International Airport.
