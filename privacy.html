Esplendor Resort / Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

In short: this website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ad trackers, and it does not store anything you type. If you email us, we use what you send only to handle your inquiry about the property.

Who we are

This website presents Esplendor Resort at Río Rico, 1069 Camino Caralampi, Río Rico, Arizona 85648. It is published by MC Business Group LLC, the property owner (“we,” “us”). Questions about this policy go to sales@esplendorproject.com.

What the site collects

The site itself collects nothing about you:

  • There are no user accounts or logins.
  • We do not set cookies and do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • The inquiry form on the home page does not send or save anything. It only builds a draft email and opens it in your own email app, and nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email.
  • The income sketch, maps, 3D tour and other tools run entirely in your browser.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive whatever you include, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message. We use it to reply, send offering materials, arrange confidentiality agreements and tours, and keep a record of the sale process.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with people who help with the sale, such as our brokers, attorneys, accountants and lenders, or when the law requires it.

Services the pages load

To work, the pages load files from a few outside services. Like any website, each of these services receives your IP address and basic browser details when your browser asks for a file. We do not send them anything else about you, and their own privacy policies apply.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may keep server logs. GitHub privacy statement
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Google Fonts privacy FAQ
  • Open-Meteo supplies the live Río Rico weather. Your browser asks it for the resort's location, not yours. Open-Meteo terms and privacy
  • jsDelivr delivers the 3D library used by the 3D tour. jsDelivr privacy policy

Links and sharing

The site links to news articles, public records and other websites. Those sites have their own policies. The share buttons are plain links: nothing loads from WhatsApp, LinkedIn or your email app until you click one.

Your choices and rights

You can browse the whole site without giving us any information. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to tell you what we hold, correct it or delete it, by writing to sales@esplendorproject.com. We will honor any other rights that the privacy laws of your state or country give you.

Security and retention

We keep inquiry emails only as long as we need them for the sale and our business records, and we take reasonable care to protect them. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

Children

This site is meant for real estate buyers, investors and their advisors. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
