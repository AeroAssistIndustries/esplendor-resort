Rose sandstone walls topped with kangura crenellations, four gilded chhatris and a fluted dome. At the heart of the house is an open court with a stepped baoli pool. On the valley side, a long terrace ends in an infinity pool.

The house is two levels set into the summit of a 49% hillside lot. It is drawn to the Town of Paradise Valley hillside rules: under the 24 ft plane that follows natural grade, under 40 ft overall, and with about 7% of the lot disturbed.

Status: schematic design SD-01, October 2026. The renderings are AI-generated design interpretations. The drawing set below is the coordinated design, built on the real parcel and LiDAR terrain.

Specifications

Structure

Cast-in-place concrete lower level set into the summit rock, retaining on the uphill sides. Concrete or ICF walls above, with steel at the great hall. The 15 ft dome is a steel frame clad in carved stone.

Stone and carving

Anchored rose sandstone veneer in coursed ashlar. Jharokhas, brackets, jaali screens and chhatris carved solid in Rajasthan, shipped in numbered crates and dry-fit before setting. Allow 12 to 18 months for carving.

Roofs

Flat roofs behind the kangura parapet, cool membrane under stone pavers, internal drains to cisterns. 44 solar modules, about 18.9 kW, kept below the parapet line.

Glazing

Low-E, low-reflectance glass in slim black steel frames, set deep in cusped arches.

Climate

Zoned VRF heat pumps with dedicated outdoor air and energy recovery. Radiant floors under the stone.

Power

APS service, the 18.9 kW roof array, battery storage and a natural-gas standby generator.

Water and wastewater

City water, residential fire sprinklers and rainwater cisterns. No sewer: an engineered onsite system with advanced treatment, sized for four suites plus staff.

Lighting

Warm, shielded, low-output fixtures only: lanterns, step lights and niches. Nothing uplights the facade, in line with the hillside lighting rules.

Pools

A 12 by 12 ft baoli pool at the bottom of the stepped court and a 20 by 9 ft infinity pool on the northeast terrace.

Landscape

Native desert kept wherever it is untouched. Disturbed edges revegetated with salvaged cacti and natives. Citrus, bougainvillea and jasmine inside the walls only.

Hillside fit

Overall height 37 ft 6 in against the 40 ft limit. Every roof point sits under the 24 ft plane, with the tightest margin 4 in at the northeast corner. Earth-tone stone, LRV 38 or lower.

Access

Elevator between the two levels and a step-free route from the gate to the court and the primary suite.

Before going further

  1. Commission an ALTA and topographic survey to confirm the LiDAR terrain the drawings are built on. The 24 ft plane margin is only 4 in at the northeast corner.
  2. Get a geotechnical report and a perc test for the onsite wastewater field.
  3. Confirm hillside status, utility capacity and fire flow with the Town, and book a Hillside Building Committee pre-application meeting.
  4. Engage an Arizona-registered architect with Paradise Valley hillside approvals, and a Rajasthan carving workshop to mock up one jharokha.
