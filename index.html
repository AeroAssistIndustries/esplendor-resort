What's around it

Ten miles from the border, fifteen from the Hermosa mine

    The opportunity

    A finished resort site with the hard part already done

    Esplendor opened in 1970 as a Spanish Colonial resort on a mesa with 360-degree views of the Santa Cruz Valley. A kitchen fire closed it in 2015, and it sat vacant until the current ownership group bought it in 2021.

    Since then the owners have demolished and cleared the fire-damaged areas, cleaned the full property, repaired roofs and corridors, and commissioned a complete remodeling plan set with architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and civil drawings. The guest buildings, ballroom, pool, tennis courts and grounds remain in place.

    After five years of ownership, the group is ready to hand the project to a buyer who will complete the renovation and reopen it, or to bring in a capital partner and finish it together.

    The area

    A border economy with a mine coming online

    Santa Cruz County runs on cross-border trade, produce logistics and federal agencies. The Hermosa mine adds the largest private investment in southern Arizona's history, about 15 miles from the resort.

    ~900Construction workers at Hermosa today, plus about 260 permanent staff

    49,000Santa Cruz County population; Río Rico about 19,600
    93°FAverage July high at 3,500 ft, 15–20°F cooler than Phoenix

    Mine workforce

    South32 expects first production in early 2028 and full production in 2030. Crews, contractors, consultants and visiting staff need rooms now, and the operation is planned to run for decades.

    Border trade

    The Mariposa Port of Entry is Arizona's main commercial gateway with Mexico and handles a large share of the fresh produce the U.S. imports in winter. Produce brokers and logistics firms cluster in Nogales and Río Rico.

    Federal agencies

    Customs and Border Protection, Border Patrol and other agencies staff the Nogales ports year round and use short- and long-term lodging, meeting rooms and food service.

    Leisure and events

    Weddings and retreats, golf at Tubac, birding along the Santa Cruz River, Sonoita wine country, and Mexican travelers heading north.
